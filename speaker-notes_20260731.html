Connecting AI Technology with Practical Applications

对应观众页：presentation_20260731.html（8 slides） · Arvione AI · Louis Lee
语气：自信、克制、偏 builder / product · 不要讲成「帮中国 AI 企业进日本」

Core Message

Arvione connects AI technology with practical applications through product development, localization, and deployment.

3 Lines to Remember

  • Arvione is a Tokyo-based AI product development company.
  • We connect AI technology with product experience and practical use.
  • Localization is part of product development.
Slide 01 Connecting AI Technology with Practical Applications 50–60 sec

Purpose

开场，清晰抛出主题，不要堆信息。

中文要点

  • 开场不要太重，不要批评别的团队
  • 先说 AI 发展很快，接下来更关键的是如何进入实际应用
  • 顺势引出 Arvione 的工作方向
不要过度：不要上来讲市场进入服务、不要讲中日对比。

Suggested English

“AI technology is advancing rapidly.”

“At the same time, an important question is how we connect these capabilities with practical applications.”

“This is the space where Arvione works.”

Transition → “So the next question is: what does it take to turn AI capability into practical use?”
Slide 02 Why This Matters ~1 min

Purpose

解释「AI 能力」与「实际应用」之间的缺口。

中文要点

  • 强调不只是模型本身
  • 还有产品设计、系统集成、本地化、部署
  • 语气温和，不说「很多人失败了」
  • 重点是「实际采用需要更多层的工作」
可指着流程条讲：Technology → Design → Integration → Localization → Deployment

Suggested English

“AI capability alone is often only one part of the picture.”

“Practical application also depends on product design, integration, localization, and deployment.”

“Real adoption happens when these layers work together.”

Transition → “That brings me to what Arvione actually does.”
Slide 03 Who We Are ~1 min

Purpose

用一句话把公司定义清楚、可信。

中文要点

  • 用一句话定义：Tokyo-based AI product development company
  • 不要讲太多泛服务
  • 强调 AI-powered software, intelligent interfaces, practical solutions
  • 右侧面板可带一句官网语气：Engineering + Market Fit
避免：consulting firm / market entry agency 的自我定位。

Suggested English

“Arvione is a Tokyo-based AI product development company.”

“We develop AI-powered software, intelligent interfaces, and practical AI solutions.”

“Our focus is on connecting technology with actual business and user environments.”

Transition → “We usually think about our work in four steps.”
Slide 04 What We Do — Discover / Build / Localize / Deploy ~1 min

Purpose

方法论页：让人记住你们怎么工作。

中文要点

  • 这一页是你的框架页
  • 不要过度展开
  • 每个词讲一句就够
  • Localize = 产品体验适配，不是单纯翻译，也不是「帮别人进市场」

Suggested English

“We often think about our work in four steps: Discover, Build, Localize, and Deploy.”

“First, we understand the user and the workflow.”

“Then we build the product and AI experience.”

“We localize the experience for the target environment.”

“And finally, we connect it to practical use and deployment.”

Transition → “Let me show a few examples.”
Slide 05 Rokid Card / Rokid Inspection ~1.5 min

Purpose

展示「设备 + AI + 工作流」的落地例子。

中文要点

  • Rokid Card：眼镜、识别、手机、后端、实际使用流程；可短播视频
  • 不要陷入技术细节
  • Rokid Inspection：巡检场景、设备交互、数据采集、业务支持
  • 两个案例都讲成「连接技术与实际场景」
时间紧：Card 约 50s（含视频），Inspection 约 40s。

Suggested English — Rokid Card

“One example is Rokid Card.”

“This workflow uses smart glasses, AI recognition, mobile interaction, and backend integration.”

“The goal is to turn physical business card information into structured, searchable, and manageable data.”

Suggested English — Rokid Inspection

“Another example is an inspection-oriented use case.”

“Here, AI and smart-device interaction support practical field operations.”

“It is not only about recognition, but also about fitting into a real operational workflow.”

Transition → “Beyond connected devices, we also work on application-oriented AI experiences.”
Slide 06 AI HR Mock Interview Deployment ~1 min

Purpose

展示纯软件侧的应用型 AI 能力——你们不只做硬件相关。

中文要点

  • 强调对用户实际有帮助的应用
  • 面试训练 / HR support / interactive AI experience
  • 强调交互体验和使用场景
  • 体现 conversational AI + product design + deployment

Suggested English

“We also work on applied AI experiences in software.”

“One example is an AI HR mock interview scenario.”

“This kind of application combines conversational AI, product design, and workflow-oriented deployment.”

“It shows how AI can be shaped into a practical user experience.”

Transition → “We are also interested in how emerging technologies can be adapted to more human-centered environments.”
Slide 07 Unitree Elderly Care POC ~1.2 min

Purpose

展示机器人 / 人本场景下的务实探索能力。

中文要点

  • 一定说 POC，不要讲太满
  • 强调 caregiving context, perception, support, real environment
  • 重点体现开发与场景理解能力
  • 可短播视频，但先口头定性「这是 POC」
严禁暗示已大规模部署或已商业量产落地。

Suggested English

“We are also exploring practical AI in robotics-related contexts.”

“This elderly care scenario is a POC that looks at how robotics, perception, and workflow design can support caregiving environments.”

“For us, this is another example of connecting advanced technology with real human settings.”

Transition → “Across these examples, a common theme appears.”
Slide 08 Closing ~1 min

Purpose

收束定位，开放合作，不要太像销售。

中文要点

  • 总结三件事：产品开发、本地化、实际部署
  • 回到标题：connect AI with practical applications
  • 结尾对 founder / enterprise / partner / investor 都开放
  • 语气从容即可，不必硬推下一步会议

Suggested English

“Across all of these examples, the common theme is connection.”

“Practical AI happens when technology, product design, and context come together.”

“At Arvione, we focus on building that connection through product development, localization, and deployment.”

“We look forward to collaborating with founders, enterprises, and partners building practical AI applications.”

Ending → “Thank you very much.”

Q&A Preparation

可能被问到的问题 · 建议短答（中文备忘 + 英文可直接说）

What makes Arvione different?
我们不只停留在概念或咨询报告，而是把 AI 做成可交互、可部署、贴合工作流的产品与方案。
“We focus on connecting AI capability with product experience and practical deployment—not just advice or demos.”
Are you a consulting company or a product company?
我们是 AI product development company。咨询式理解场景是工作的一部分，但核心是开发和落地。
“We are a product development company. Understanding the context matters, but our core work is building and deploying.”
Do you focus on software or hardware?
两边都做连接：软件体验、设备交互、以及软件 + 设备 + 工作流的整合。
“Both sides of the connection—software experiences, device interaction, and software + device + workflow integration.”
Are these commercial projects or explorations?
混合。有面向实际使用的应用开发，也有明确标注为 POC 的探索（如介护场景）。会诚实区分。
“A mix. Some are applied product work; some, like the elderly care scenario, are clearly framed as POCs.”
What kinds of partners are you looking for?
希望与 founders、企业创新团队、以及正在把 AI 做成实际产品的伙伴合作——尤其是重视产品、本地化与部署的团队。
“Founders, enterprises, and partners who want to turn AI into practical products—especially where product design, localization, and deployment matter.”

排练建议：先按时间表过一遍英文主句，再加视频；总时长卡在 9.5–10 分钟。
